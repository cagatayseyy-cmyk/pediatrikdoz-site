FAQ

What is Pediatric Dose?

A mobile app for pediatricians: weight- and indication-based doses for 130 drugs, age-based reference ranges, 13 clinical calculators, 8 clinical scores, an immunisation schedule and corticosteroid equivalences. Offline, no ads.

Is it free?

Free to download. 20 core drugs and the core calculators and scores are free; other content can be viewed a limited number of times per day. Premium (monthly, 6-month or one-time lifetime) unlocks everything.

How is a pediatric dose calculated?

Per kilogram of body weight (mg/kg/dose or mg/kg/day), capped at the maximum dose and divided by the dosing frequency. The app does the maths and shows mL by preparation. It is a supporting tool; the final decision rests with the clinician.

Does it collect patient data?

No. Values are processed on the device only. See the privacy policy.

Important: Pediatric Dose is a clinical decision support tool for healthcare professionals. It does not replace clinical judgement, current guidelines or official product information. All responsibility for diagnosis, treatment and drug use rests with the user.
